Optimization recommendations

Ordered by safety first, then by output you could win back

  1. 1

    Have P-09’s connector inspected

    Turn the array off at the isolator before anyone touches the wiring. A technician should check the connector and cable.

    P-09Est. +180 W

  2. 2

    Investigate P-10 overheating

    Do not touch the panel. Ask an installer to look for a hot spot, a failed bypass diode or blocked airflow behind it.

    P-10Est. +100 W

  3. 3

    Clean the dusty panel (P-06)

    Use a soft brush and clean water in the early morning or evening. Avoid cold water on hot glass.

    P-06Est. +70 W

  4. 4

    Check why P-08 is producing less than expected

    It is clean and fault-free but 18% under target. Look for new shadows from branches or antennas, and for bird droppings.

    P-08Est. +60 W

  5. 5

    Run high-energy appliances at peak solar

    Output is expected to be strongest between 11 AM and 3 PM. Run the washing machine, dishwasher, water heater or EV charger then to use more of your own solar power.

    Load shiftingHigher self-use

  6. 6

    Add P-04 and P-05 to the cleaning visit

    Both have medium dust. Cleaning them with P-06 stops them sliding into high dust.
